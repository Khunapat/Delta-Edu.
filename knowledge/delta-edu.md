# δelta Edu.: what Delta always knows

Edit this file to teach Delta more (then redeploy). This repo is PUBLIC: only put
things here you'd put on a website. Anything private (equity, pay ladder, prices
not yet agreed, open partner decisions, the roadmap) goes on the web board's
**Notes** page instead, which lives in the team's private Google Sheet. Never put
phone numbers or emails anywhere.

- δelta Edu. ("Delta Edu") is a Thai tutoring institution. Tagline: "Small things create big change." δ means change: small gains in understanding add up to real academic progress.
- Two brands, given equal focus:
  - δelta Edu. (tag "Uni"): university level. Live online small groups for undergraduates, Chulalongkorn first. University foundations (Calculus I-III, Differential Equations, General Physics I-II, General Chemistry I-II, Basic Organic Chemistry) and Chemical Engineering core courses (Thermodynamics I first, then Mass and Energy Balance, ChE Math, Kinetics and Reactor Design, Process Dynamics and Control).
  - δelta Edu. LITE (tag "LITE"): international secondary level, the new name of AlDenté Academy. Tracks: SAT (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, plus the "6 Days 7 Tests" intensive), GED (Math, Science, Language Arts, Social Studies), IGCSE (Physics 0625 first, one-on-one), A-Level (planned).
- Teaching approach: why before how. Three equal outcomes in every course: clear concepts (start from what the student knows), independent thinking (why a method works), academic results (tied to real course objectives and exam-style practice). Professional yet approachable, "as comfortable as asking a friend".
- Team (nicknames): Best (founder, academic lead), Jai (partner, bot admin), Kiak (partner and tutor), Pure (partner, marketing and operations). Tutors teach for one of the two brands.
- Tools: the Delta LINE bot (tasks, classes, decisions, reminders, team questions) and the δelta Edu. web board, both on the same private Google Sheet.
