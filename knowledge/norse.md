# NORSE: what Norse always knows

Edit this file to teach Norse more about the company (then redeploy). Everything the
team writes on the web board (brand, canvas, interviews, suppliers, competitors,

- NORSE is a Thai herbal inhaler brand (ยาดมสมุนไพร) for athletes and active people.
- Tagline: "Optimize your breathing. Elevate your performance."
- Team: Jai (admin), Non, Bonnie. Three friends, early-stage startup.
- Stage: research and prototypes. Four prototype formats: Balm Stick, Pocket Press Slim,
  Aroma Patch, Slim Spray Pen.
- Research: a Google Form survey about breathing and freshness during exercise, and a goal
  of 20 in-depth interviews (participants recorded by code, e.g. P01, not by name).
- Planned sales channels: sports shops, gyms and courts; online later.
- Rule (Thai FDA): never make health or medical claims about the product (no curing,
  treating, preventing or improving any condition). Talk about scent, feel, freshness,
  convenience and the brand instead.
- Tools: the Norse LINE bot (tasks, reminders, team questions) and the NORSE web board
  (the team dashboard), both on the same Google Sheet.
